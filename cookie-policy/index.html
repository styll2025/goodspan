Cookie Policy

Effective Date: 21 July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how The Good Span uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

The Good Span is operated by PALIX UNLIMITED LTD, a private limited company registered in Malta under Company Registration Number C101345.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function correctly, remember your preferences and provide information about how the site is used.

How we use cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Keep you signed in to your account
  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Improve the performance of our website
  • Understand how visitors use The Good Span
  • Help us identify technical issues and improve the user experience

We do not use cookies to sell your personal information.

Types of cookies we use

Essential Cookies. These cookies are necessary for the website to function correctly. They enable features such as:

  • User authentication
  • Security
  • Session management
  • Navigation

Without these cookies, some parts of the website may not work properly.

Analytics Cookies. Analytics cookies help us understand how people use The Good Span. For example, they may tell us:

  • which pages are visited most often;
  • how long people spend on the site;
  • whether users encounter errors.

This information helps us improve the experience for everyone.

Preference Cookies. These cookies remember choices you make, such as language or display preferences, so you don't need to set them each time you visit.

Marketing Cookies. At launch, The Good Span does not use advertising or marketing cookies to track users across websites. If this changes in the future, we will update this Cookie Policy and request your consent where required.

Managing cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect how The Good Span functions.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time as we introduce new features or where required by law.
