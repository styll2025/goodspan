Example Good Span

Maria, 34

Busy professional

Maria, you told us you'd like to eat well and feel less stressed, so that's where we're starting.

You already have strong foundations in sleep, movement and connection, and we'll build on what already works.

This Good Span starts with

Two practices · Nutrition · Mind · about 60 minutes a week

Starting Position

A clear picture of where Maria is today across six connected dimensions: what is already supporting her wellbeing, and where a small change could help most.

Focus nowWorth improvingGoing well

NutritionYour focus

You eat 1–2 portions of vegetables and fruit a day, and two of your main meals include a source of protein.

SleepWell supported

You sleep 7 to 8 hours on a typical night, and sleep itself isn't usually a struggle.

MovementStaying active

You're active on 4 days a week, for about 40 minutes each time: around 160 minutes a week in all.

MindYour focus

You don't smoke or vape, and you drink alcohol 2 to 4 times a month, usually 1 to 2 drinks at a time.

Where a small change could go a long way

Focus

Nutrition

You'd like support with eating more plants and fibre and reducing highly processed foods.

You usually eat 1–2 portions of vegetables and fruit a day, and at least 5 are recommended. Processed foods are part of 3–5 days of your week, and swapping some for fresher options is linked with better long-term health.

What’s already going well

  • Cooking 3 to 5 main meals a week at home.
  • Including protein in two of your main meals.
  • Keeping sugary drinks and added salt occasional.

Focus

Mind

You'd like support with managing stress and switching off and relaxing.

You already make time for things you enjoy and spend time in nature. A few simple, regular ways to unwind can help you switch off more easily.

What’s already going well

  • Making time for hobbies and activities you enjoy.
  • Spending 2–3 hours a week in nature.

We build on these habits instead of asking her to start from scratch.

First two practices

The first steps for the month ahead, chosen around what matters to Maria and the time she has.

Nutrition

01 · Balanced plate

Add a portion of vegetables or fruit to one meal a day, 3 days a week.

Time

3 days a week. It fits into meals you already have, without setting extra time aside.

Make it work for you

Good choices include apples and pears, citrus fruits, leafy greens, broccoli, cabbage and salads.

Why this practice

You told us you want to work on how you eat, and you usually eat 1–2 portions of vegetables and fruit a day. At least 5 are recommended.

What supports this: Aune et al. (2017)

Mind

02 · Yoga for stress

Join a beginner yoga class or follow a beginner-friendly session for about 60 minutes, once a week.

Time

For about 60 minutes, once a week. About 60 minutes a week in all.

Make it work for you

Choose a gentle style that includes postures, breathing and relaxation, and tell the teacher about any injuries. Move within a comfortable range and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or unwell.

Why this practice

You told us managing stress matters to you. Yoga that combines postures, breathing and relaxation is a practical way to work on it, and you can do it at home.

What supports this: Schleinzer et al. (2024)

The six-month journey

A Good Span is a starting point, not a fixed prescription. Each month, Maria reflects on what’s working, adapts what isn’t and decides what comes next. New practices join one at a time, once the earlier ones feel familiar.

  1. Month 1 · Begin

    Get familiar with the first two practices and find ways to make them part of the week.

    About 60 minutes a week

  2. Month 2 · Build

    Keep the practices that are working and, when ready, add:

    • NutritionPlant varietyTry a plant food you don't usually eat once a week.
    • NutritionBeans and lentilsAdd beans, lentils or chickpeas to a meal once a week.

    About 60 minutes a week in all

  3. Month 3 · Refine

    Review progress and adjust the practices to fit routines, priorities and experience. If it feels right, add:

    • MindSavouringPause to savour a pleasant moment as it happens for 1–2 minutes, once a day.

    About 60 minutes a week in all, within the 1–2 hours you told us you have

  4. Months 4–5 · Deepen

    Explore the next practices in the focus areas, building on what’s been learned so far.

  5. Month 6 · Consolidate

    Identify the habits that matter most and make them easier to sustain beyond the six months.

  6. After six months · New Position

    Revisit the six dimensions to see how habits and priorities have evolved since the Starting Position, review the New Position with the Pilot and decide what to focus on next.

    Two possible next directions

    • Plant-rich eating could help you add more vegetables, fruit, whole grains, beans and lentils to your week.
    • Mindfulness and meditation could help you build calm and focus, through meditation, breathing and yoga.

    Or keep going with what’s working. Maintaining a habit that supports wellbeing is progress too.
