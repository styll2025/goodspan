A personalised way
to make longevity part

Build a six-month Good Span around what matters most to you, with practices that fit your life and change as you progress. Your Pilot, a trained human longevity guide, is there from your first conversation, and you choose how much ongoing guidance you’d like.

Find your Good Span
01

Step one

Find your Starting Position.

Begin with a free personal evaluation of your habits, priorities and everyday life across six connected dimensions: nutrition, sleep, movement, mind, connection and prevention.

Tell us what’s already working, what you’d like to improve and how much time you have. You’ll receive your Starting Position: a clear picture of your strengths, your opportunities and where it makes sense to start.

02

Step two

Build your Good Span.

Your Good Span begins with a small number of science-backed practices, chosen around your goals, current habits and available time. Some help you build something new, while others strengthen what you already do or help you do it more consistently.

03

Step three

Meet your Pilot.

Your six-month Good Span begins with a welcome call with your Pilot, who helps you put it into practice. Together, you review your Starting Position, talk through your recommended practices and refine them around your goals, available time and everyday life.

Ask questions, raise anything that doesn’t feel right, and agree on practices that feel realistic for you.

04

Step four

Review and adapt your Good Span.

Your Good Span changes as you put it into practice. Each month, look at what’s working, what feels difficult and what needs to change. For each practice, you can:

Continue
Keep building consistency, or keep doing what works.
Build on
Take a further step when you feel ready.
Adjust
Change how often or how you do it, so it fits your life.
Replace
Try another practice that better suits your priorities.
05

Step five

Choose your human support.

After your welcome call, choose how much human guidance you want, from focused conversations with your Pilot when you need them to regular support throughout your Good Span.

Your Pilot helps you make sense of what’s working, work through what isn’t, and adjust your practices as your life and priorities change. When deeper expertise would help, they connect you with the right specialist.

06

Step six

Discover your New Position.

After six months, revisit the six dimensions and review your New Position with your Pilot. Compare it with your Starting Position to see what has changed, what has become easier and where you’d like to focus next.

Your New Position then becomes the starting point for your next Good Span, shaped by everything you’ve learned.

The research behind
The Good Span.

Every practice in The Good Span is backed by scientific research on how to live a longer, healthier life.

Find your Good Span

From our research library

We have more than 200 practices across nutrition, sleep, movement, mind, connection and prevention. Many build on the everyday habits with the strongest evidence for a longer, healthier life: sleeping well, moving regularly, eating well, nurturing relationships and keeping up with preventive care.

Nutrition

What your gut bacteria can tell us about eating more plants

McDonald et al. (2018), mSystems

In one of the largest studies of the gut microbiome, people who ate more than 30 different plant foods a week had more diverse gut bacteria than people who ate 10 or fewer. Variety seems to matter as much as quantity, and adding one new plant food each week is an easy way to build it.

Connection

The 30-minute rule that helped college students feel less lonely

Hunt et al. (2018), Journal of Social and Clinical Psychology

143 students were randomly asked either to limit Facebook, Instagram and Snapchat to 10 minutes each a day or to carry on as usual. After three weeks, the group that cut back felt significantly less lonely and less depressed.

Movement

122,000 treadmill tests later, fitness still predicted who lived longer

Mandsager et al. (2018), JAMA Network Open

Across 122,007 adults followed for around eight years, every step up in measured fitness was linked to a lower risk of death, with no upper limit. Low fitness carried a risk comparable to smoking or diabetes.

Sleep

60,000 participants, seven years, one clear signal: regular sleep timing beats sleep duration for survival

Windred et al. (2024), Sleep

Using wrist-tracker data from 60,977 people, researchers found that those with more regular sleep and wake times had a 20 to 48 percent lower risk of death than those with the least regular sleep. Keeping a steady routine predicted long-term health better than the number of hours slept.

Our story.

Hello, I’m Joël, founder of The Good Span.

I came close to dying when I was 29. That experience changed how I thought about the years ahead. A long life matters because of what we can do, feel and share along the way.

Health is part of that, along with curiosity, relationships, pleasure, purpose and discovery.

I created The Good Span to help people look after their health while making room for the things that make life worth living.

I’d be delighted to welcome you to The Good Span.

If you’d like to know more about my story or The Good Span, write to me at joel@thegoodspan.com.

Longevity should add to life.

We think about the years ahead because of what they can hold: time with the people you love, things you still want to learn, places you’d like to see. Our practices are designed to fit into your everyday life, so looking after your health leaves you more room for all of that.

Personal guidance, made possible by technology.

We want personalised longevity to be within reach for more people, and technology helps us do that. A good recommendation still starts with getting to know you: what you care about, what you enjoy, how full your life is and what you feel ready to change.

That’s why every member has a Pilot who listens before advising, respects your choices and knows when to bring in a specialist.

We believe we age better together.

Some of the best parts of life are shared: a walk with a friend, discovering something new with someone, or simply having someone to talk to. That company brings pleasure and support to everyday life, so The Good Span brings members together around shared interests, including a curiosity about living well.

Born in Lisbon, open to members across Europe.

Lisbon inspires us with its outdoor life, shared tables and people seeking a more balanced way of living.

That way of life shapes The Good Span: practical habits, human guidance and meaningful connections, with room for curiosity, pleasure and discovery.

From Lisbon, The Good Span is growing across Europe. Members connect online today, and we’re adding more local gatherings, partnerships and experiences in more places.
