Start with where
you are today.

Our free personal evaluation looks at your habits, priorities and everyday life across six connected dimensions: nutrition, sleep, movement, mind, connection and prevention.

You’ll discover your Starting Position and the practices that matter most for you, then review them with your Pilot to shape the six-month Good Span that is right for you.

Find my Good Span

Tell us about your everyday life.

What matters to you? What’s already working? What would you like to change?

Alongside your current habits, we ask about your goals, the time you have, your preferences and what feels realistic for you. There are no right or wrong answers, and the more honest the picture, the more relevant your practices will be.

Discover your Starting Position.

Your answers shape your Starting Position, a snapshot of where you are today across the six dimensions.

Build your Good Span.

Your Good Span starts from there, with a small number of practices chosen around what matters most to you, your current habits and the time you really have.

Each practice tells you what to do, how often and why it was picked for you. You’ll go through them with your Pilot, and you can adapt them as you find out what works.

Choose how you’re supported.

Your six-month Good Span begins with a welcome call with your Pilot to review your Starting Position and refine your practices.

After that, choose how much ongoing guidance you’d like:

Self

Follow your Good Span independently, and book additional Pilot or specialist consultations when you want them.

Core

Stay on track with monthly Pilot reviews and support between them.

Concierge

Work more closely with your Pilot, with specialist consultations and help coordinating services.

Prefer to try it first? A one-month Self trial is available for €25, without Pilot support.
