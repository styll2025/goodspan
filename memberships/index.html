Your six-month Good Span includes

Whichever membership you choose, you have your own Good Span.

  • Your Starting Position: a clear picture of where you are today across the six dimensions
  • Personalised practices focused on what matters most for you
  • A six-month Good Span that evolves as you progress
  • A welcome call with your Pilot to review and refine your Good Span
  • Member App and Practice Journal
  • Monthly Longevity Live, with replay
  • Thoughtful member introductions and gatherings
  • Access to curated specialists, products and services at preferred member rates
  • A review of your New Position with your Pilot after six months

Human guidance when you need it.

Your Pilot is a trained human longevity guide who helps you make sense of your Good Span, put it into practice and adapt it as you progress. When deeper expertise can help, they connect you with the right specialist.

Self

More ways to support
your Good Span.

Preferred member rates

Access carefully selected specialists, assessments, services and products at preferred rates negotiated by The Good Span. Our network and member benefits will keep growing with our community.

Quarterly Refuel

Concierge members receive €100 each quarter, a little extra to help you keep progressing. Use it towards selected services and products, and explore the options with your Pilot, from a sleep consultation to a light therapy lamp or an organic food basket.

Longevity Live

Join our monthly live conversation with a leading specialist in longevity and wellbeing. Hear what matters, explore the latest thinking and ask your questions, or watch the replay whenever it suits you.

Available across Europe.

Access your Good Span and get guidance from your Pilot wherever you live in Europe. As our communities grow, connect with fellow members online and meet in person through local gatherings, partnerships and experiences.
